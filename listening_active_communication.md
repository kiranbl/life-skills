# Listening and Active Communication

## Question 1: What are the steps or strategies for Active Listening?

1. Pay full attention to the speaker.
2. Avoid distractions and focus on the conversation.
3. Do not interrupt; let the speaker finish.
4. Maintain comfortable eye contact and use positive body language.
5. Use encouraging words like "I see" or "Please continue."
6. Ask questions when something is unclear.
7. Take notes during important discussions.
8. Summarize key points to check your understanding.

## Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

1. Listen more and speak less.
2. Focus on what the speaker is personally trying to say.
3. Repeat the main idea in your own words to check your understanding.
4. Try to understand the speaker's feelings, not just the facts.
5. Look at the situation from the speaker's point of view rather than your own.
6. Respond with empathy and respect, without judging the speaker.

## Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

Sometimes I get distracted or I may also miss small details when someone explains a topic quickly.

## Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

I can focus on the speaker and wait until they finish before responding. I will take notes, ask questions when I have doubts and summarize important points to confirm my understanding.

## Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication in daily life?

I sometimes become passive when I disagree with someone but hesitate to express my opinion, especially in a group or with someone more experienced.

## Question 6: When do you switch to Aggressive communication in daily life?

I may become aggressive when I feel that I am not being heard. At such times, I might speak out angrily.

## Question 7: When do you switch to Passive-Aggressive communication in daily life?

Sometimes, when I am upset but do not want to talk about it directly, I may become quiet or make them understand in a way the other person understand.

## Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive?

1. Understand and name my feelings before speaking.
2. Identify what I need and explain it clearly.
3. Use polite and direct words, such as "I feel" and "I need."
4. Practise speaking up in small, everyday situations.
5. Keep my voice calm and my body language confident.
6. Listen to the other person's point of view.
7. Raise concerns early instead of letting frustration build up.
8. Say "no" respectfully when I cannot agree to something.
