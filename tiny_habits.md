
# Tiny Habits

## Question 1: What was the most interesting idea from the Tiny Habits video?

The most interesting idea was that small habits can lead to big changes. Starting with something simple like flossing one tooth can help us build a lasting habit.

## Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make new habits easier?

B = MAP means Behaviour = Motivation + Ability + Prompt.

- M (Motivation): Have a reason to do the habit.
- A (Ability): Make the habit easy to perform.
- P (Prompt): Have a reminder to do it.

For example, I can practise coding for 10 minutes after going home every day.

## Question 3: Why is it important to Shine or Celebrate?

Celebrating small achievements makes us feel happy and successful. This positive feeling encourages us to repeat the habit until it becomes part of our routine.

## Question 4: What was the most interesting idea from the 1% Better Every Day video?

The story of the British cycling team was interesting. It showed how making small improvements in different areas can lead to great results over time.

## Question 5: What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book explains that habits help shape our identity. Instead of saying "I want to become a good programmer" I should think "I am someone who practises coding every day."

## Question 8: Pick one habit you want to do more of. How will you build it?

1. Keep my laptop ready to practise.
2. Start with just 10 minutes daily.
3. Practise after breakfast to build a routine.
