# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where we explain a concept in simple words, identify gaps in our understanding and improve by learning those parts again.

## Question 2: What was the most interesting story or idea for you?

I found the story of Thomas Edison interesting. He used to relax while holding ball bearings and when he fell asleep they would drop and wake him up. This helped him come up with new ideas. It made me realize that taking breaks can also help us solve problems.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Focused (Active) Mode:** Thinking with full concentration to understand a concept or solve a problem.
- **Diffuse Mode:** A relaxed state of thinking where our brain makes connections and finds new ideas, even when we are not actively focusing on the problem.

## Question 4: What are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Deconstruct the skill into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to self-correct.
3. Remove barriers and distractions to practice.
4. Practice for at least 20 hours.

## Question 5: What actions can you take going forward to improve your learning process?

1. Avoid distractions and give my full attention while learning.
2. Break complex topics into smaller, manageable parts.
3. Understand concepts clearly instead of just memorizing them.
4. Explain concepts in my own words to check my understanding.
5. Practice coding regularly and apply what I learn.
6. Take short breaks when stuck and approach problems with a fresh mind.
7. Manage my time properly and complete tasks before deadlines.
8. Communicate my doubts and difficulties proactively.
