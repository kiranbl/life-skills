
# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1: Summarize the Grit video.

Grit means staying focused on our goals and not giving up. Success depends on hard work and determination rather than just talent.

## Question 2: Summarize the Growth Mindset video.

A growth mindset means believing we can improve through effort and practice. We should learn from mistakes and see challenges as opportunities to grow.

## Question 3: What is Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and efforts can influence our success.

The key point is to take responsibility for our actions and focus on what we can control instead of blaming others.

## Question 4: What are the key points to build a Growth Mindset?

1. Believe in your ability to figure things out.
2. Question your assumptions.
3. Create your own learning plan.
4. Accept struggles as part of learning.

## Question 5: What are your ideas to build a Growth Mindset?

1. Practise coding regularly.
2. Learn from my mistakes.
3. Try solving problems before asking for help.
4. Accept feedback and improve myself.
5. Keep learning new skills.
6. Stay consistent and never give up easily.
